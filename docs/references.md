## Academic Literature

### Original CarbonXS Model & Dissertation

1. **H. Shi**, *Disordered Carbons and Battery Applications*, Ph.D. Dissertation, Simon Fraser University (1993) — describes the model CarbonXS uses in the context of carbon materials for Li-ion batteries.  
   [Download PDF from Simon Fraser University Summit](https://summit.sfu.ca/_flysystem/fedora/sfu_migrate/5690/b1523325x.pdf)

2. **H. Shi, J. N. Reimers, and J. R. Dahn**, *"Structure-refinement program for disordered carbons,"* *Journal of Applied Crystallography* (1993) — describes the carbon structure-refinement program.  
   [View article PDF (IUCr)](https://journals.iucr.org/j/issues/1993/06/00/hw0018/hw0018.pdf)

### CarbonXS GUI Publication

3. **L.-K. Tsui et al.**, *"CarbonXS GUI: a graphical front-end for CarbonXS,"* *Journal of Applied Crystallography* (2017).  
   [View paper (IUCr)](https://scripts.iucr.org/cgi-bin/paper?S1600576717015035)

> **Citing CarbonXS GUI:** Please cite the 2017 *Journal of Applied Crystallography* paper when citing CarbonXS GUI, along with the original CarbonXS refinement paper (Shi, Reimers, & Dahn, 1993).
